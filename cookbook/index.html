Before you cook

Healthy should not take money, time, or a culinary degree.

This cookbook was built around that one idea. Every recipe uses ingredients you can find at any grocery store, keeps prep and cook time under 30 minutes, and is designed to fit into a full week of eating for under $50.

The Meal Prep section matters most. Making batch chicken, rice and roasted vegetables on Sunday afternoon is the move that makes the rest of the week easy.

Every recipe lists calories and macros per serving, an estimated cost per serving, and a link to a ready-made Instacart shopping list for its ingredients.

Nutritional values are estimates based on USDA data and vary with brands, cooking methods and portion sizes. iEatz Healthy may earn a commission on Instacart orders placed through these links.

5 habits that make this book work

  1. Cook rice and chicken on Sunday. It saves 20 minutes every weekday night.
  2. Buy proteins in bulk and freeze what you don’t use that week.
  3. Keep canned beans, canned tomatoes and frozen shrimp in stock, always.
  4. Read the tip at the bottom of each recipe. It is usually the most useful line on the page.
  5. Scale recipes up 2x and eat leftovers. Most of these get better on day two.

Garlic Spinach Scrambled Eggs

  • 13 min total
  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Serves 1
Calories
310
Protein
21g
Carbs
5g
Fat
23g
Per serving
~$1.65

Allergens: Eggs

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 cups fresh spinach
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat.
  2. Add minced garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Add spinach and stir until wilted, about 2 minutes.
  4. Crack eggs into the pan. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Stir gently, folding eggs over low-medium heat until just set. Serve immediately.

Tip. Pull the eggs off heat while they still look slightly wet — they finish cooking from residual heat and stay creamy.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats (dry)

  • 1 medium banana, sliced
  • 2 tbsp natural peanut butter
  • Pinch of salt
  • Optional: cinnamon to taste

Instructions

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon (sweet) or paprika (savory)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with foil or parchment.

Simple Veggie Saute Base

  • 20 min total
  • Prep 8 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Serves 4
Calories
75
Protein
3g
Carbs
10g
Fat
4g
Per serving
~$1.20

Allergens: None

Ingredients

  • 1 large bell pepper, diced
  • 1 medium zucchini, diced
  • 1 cup broccoli florets, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, and garlic powder to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Add onion and cook 3 minutes until softened.

Tip. This is your weekly veggie base. Add it to eggs in the morning, rice at lunch, or protein at dinner.
