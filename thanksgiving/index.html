How it works

Three taps, then stop thinking about it.

  1. Open the cart that matches your table. It opens on Instacart in your store.
  2. Take out anything you already have in the pantry, and change the count for a bigger or smaller table.
  3. Order for pickup or delivery, and spend the day you would have spent in the store doing anything else.
After Thursday

Tell iEatz what is left. It writes dinner.

Every big grocery run leaves ingredients behind: the second bag of green beans, the extra sweet potatoes, the stock you bought in case. Type what you have into the iEatz app and it writes recipes from those ingredients, with calories and protein estimated, and sends anything missing to Instacart.
