# iEatz Healthy — Food Photography Shot List

**Why:** the current library (`assets/photos/`) has only ~4 true plated-dish photos, so
the Recipe/Health pillars repeat images within ~2 weeks. This is the brief to commission
or source (Unsplash/Pexels/shoot) the shots that keep all four pillars fresh and on-brand.

## Look & feel (every shot)
- **Warm, candid, "captured not created"** (Instacart "human lens" + iEatz editorial-wellness):
  real, slightly imperfect, mid-moment — steam rising, a bite taken, crumbs, hands in frame.
- **Warm natural light**, paper / light-wood / neutral-linen surfaces that sit next to
  `--ie-paper #F5F2EA`. Avoid cool blue tones and clinical pure-white.
- **Negative space** for a headline and/or logo — leave a clean quadrant (usually top or
  one side). Shoot so both **4:5 (1080×1350)** and **2:3 (1000×1500)** crops work.
- **High-res: ≥ 2400px on the long edge.** (Note: `salmon.jpg` is 320×480 — too soft at pin
  size; that's the bar we're avoiding.)
- Diverse, real people for lifestyle frames. **No text/graphics baked in.** No fake app UI.
- Keep a consistent prop/palette kit across the set so the feed reads as one system.

---

## P0 — Plated dishes (unblocks Recipe + Health pillars; shoot first, ~10–12)
Each: finished dish, overhead or 3/4, one clean quadrant of negative space.
1. **Grain/Buddha bowls** — 2–3 variations (different colors/toppings) so we're not reusing one.
2. **High-protein breakfast** — eggs (shakshuka, scramble, frittata), Greek-yogurt bowl, tofu scramble.
3. **Sheet-pan dinner** — chicken + veg, or salmon + veg on the tray.
4. **Stir-fry / grain skillet** — colorful, in the pan.
5. **Tacos / wraps** — beyond the current one (fish, chicken, bean).
6. **Pasta** — one more besides pesto (tomato, veg primavera).
7. **Big salad / crunch bowl** — protein-topped, glossy.
8. **Soup or stew** — cozy, spoon in, steam.
9. **Roasted salmon / white fish** — high-res replacement for the low-res salmon.
10. **Plant-based main** — chickpea curry, lentils, tofu.
11. **Smoothie / overnight-oats / chia** — bright breakfast option.
12. **Loaded baked potato / sweet potato** — pantry-dinner hero.

## P1 — Lifestyle & candid (carousel hooks, testimonials, CTA slides; ~6–8)
- Person(s) **cooking together** in a warm kitchen — laughing, tasting off a spoon (diverse subjects).
- **Hands** chopping / plating / squeezing lime / cracking an egg (close, shallow depth).
- **Solo weeknight cook** — relaxed, one person, real kitchen.
- **Family / friends at the table**, plates down, mid-conversation.
- **Phone-in-hand in the kitchen** showing the **real** app (for device slides — real screenshots only).

## P2 — Pantry / Fridge & Grocery / Instacart pillars (~6–8)
- **Open fridge** — one well-stocked/organized, one realistically full-but-planless.
- **Pantry shelf / spice drawer** — staples, cans, jars (warm, real).
- **Grocery receipt on the counter** (the "scan your receipt" hook).
- **Produce close-ups / market flat-lay** on paper or wood.
- **Unpacking a grocery order** — bags/box on the counter, produce coming out (Instacart moment;
  no third-party logos in the photo — the brand chip is added in the template).
- **Groceries at the door / handing off** — the delivery beat.
- **Meal-prep containers** — a week portioned out (Health pillar).
