## Read this first

1. **The data skews TikTok. That's a problem for an Instagram-first plan.** Of the agent's 316 videos, 61% are TikTok, 21% YouTube and 18% Instagram. Virlo rates Instagram "too few to judge" (56 videos, 19 scored). Only 5 of the outlier videos are Reels. Treat most of the format and hook findings as TikTok findings that might carry over to Reels.
2. **Your competitors already run the playbook you'd be copying.** The agent's top insight is "explosive demand for AI-powered meal planning apps." Outlier videos promote Potto, Osta, Dish AI, Dishly, TapCook, Supercook, Mise, Dashi and PlanEat AI. Virlo flags several top videos as paid placements: madimugc444, taliameals, donna.eatssss, proteindueldaily, taylor.eats and avaartalabs.
3. **Some of the outliers are probably boosted.** Virlo's own filter note says a high Virality Score with engagement under 1% "is often a boosted post." These outliers qualify: bitewisebycrumamii (0.5%), donna.eatssss (0.7%), avaartalabs (0.1%), samuelships (0.5%), and zero_guilt_by_hela (0.3%, creator level). Don't treat their view counts as organic proof.
4. **Sounds hardly matter in this niche.** The most-used sound appears in just 2 of 316 videos. Since your business account can't use most licensed music anyway, this works in your favor.

## 6. Durable patterns

Tags: **[Data]** means it's on the dashboard. **[Inference]** means it's my read.

1. **Length: 30 seconds to 3 minutes beats 15 to 30 seconds, but only weakly.** [Data] 30 to 60s is "leaning 13% above" (104 videos), 1 to 3 min "leaning 19% above" (89), 15 to 30s "leaning 19% below" (78). Under 15s is too few to judge. Virlo says "leaning" means not enough to be sure. [Inference] Utility videos need time to show the payoff. The two Reels winners at 0:09 suggest pure recipe teasers can stay very short. Default to 30 to 60s for app videos and under 20s for single-dish teasers.

2. **First frame: a text-on-screen confession or a finished dish.** [Data] The #1 outlier opens on a face with a full on-screen sentence. The recipe outliers open on the dish name (e.g., "Garlic parmesan cheeseburger bombs"). [Inference] Lead with the outcome, not the app UI. The UI comes second.

3. **Audio: original voice wins, and trending music doesn't drive this niche.** [Data] The most-used sound appears in only 2 of 316 videos, and 7 of the top 10 sounds are original audio. [Inference] This holds up and fits your business-account limits. Build the brand on voiceover and cooking sound, not songs.

4. **Captions do the recipe's work.** [Data] Top recipe videos put the full ingredient list in the caption (arianamariaa11, eatswithmills, routevidz, proteindueldaily, ashieeeats). The Reels winners use a comment-keyword CTA: "Comment "RECIPE" for the full recipe!" (stayinupwitkiy), "Comment "Recipe" and I'll send you the full recipe card for free!!" (recipeincaption, 17M on Reels). Save CTAs show up too: "Save this recipe and thank me later 😌" (ashieeeats), "save this for your next shop! 🥙" (taylor.eats). [Inference] The comment-keyword DM fits iEatz naturally: send the recipe and the app link in one DM.

5. **Recipes and recipe-saving tools get shared. App ads get views.** [Data, share ÷ views calculated from the detail panels] Highest share rates: ninaluvstocook 4.0% (recipe saver), grooovybroth 3.6% (expiring-meat stew), ashieeeats 2.5%, goldrecipesdaily 2.4% (recipe saver). Lowest: the discovery-confession app videos, madimugc444 0.42%, donna.eatssss 0.03%, bitewisebycrumamii 0.02%. Instagram shows 0 shares on every Reel, so this is TikTok-only data. [Inference] Shares are the signal you can trust here. A video that's useful on its own (the recipe, the fridge rescue) gets passed along. A pitch doesn't.

6. **Waste and "about to go bad" is a small topic with strong engagement, and it's open.** [Data] Fridge and pantry clean-out content totals 9.9M views vs 47.4M for AI meal-planning apps. Yet grooovybroth has the second-highest share rate, and stayinupwitkiy has 7.6% engagement on Reels. [Inference] Competitors are crowding "AI meal planner." Very few apps own "rescue what's in your fridge." That's where iEatz's receipt and fridge features are different, and it's the angle least likely to go stale in two months.

7. **Series beat one-offs.** [Data] grooovybroth's "Day 470" perpetual stew, thehumblepescatarian's "Tins Made Tasty" series, dadsfoodtoday's "Dinners you'll actually make." [Inference] A repeatable series such as "Fridge Rescue #12" gives you consistent output and a format the audience learns to expect. That's what makes this a system rather than a lucky post.

8. **Paid UGC inflates this niche's leaderboard.** [Data] Virlo flags 6 of the 25 winning-format videos as paid placements, and 4 of the 25 have engagement under 1%. [Inference] Benchmark iEatz against engagement and share rate, not against 29M-view ads.
