Type-only posts built to be forwarded to a friend or partner. Sends are one of the biggest reach drivers on IG right now. Works for organic and as paid ads.

Formula

send this to whoever [specific food habit] and [the contradiction]

+ one payoff line that points back at the fridge

Rules
  • Lowercase except proper nouns
  • A real store, price or day
  • Fresh food only, it has to go bad
  • Hook under ~80 characters
  • No photos, emoji or buttons
Cadence (proposed)
  • 1 post a week in organic
  • Alternate cream and green
  • Keep 4 approved hooks queued
  • Top sends get recut as ads
